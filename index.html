Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

KaylDownUnder built the RelayIQ app as a free app. This Service is provided by KaylDownUnder at no cost and is intended for use as is.

What RelayIQ does with your data

RelayIQ does not have its own server, does not create user accounts, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to KaylDownUnder.

If you use the optional "Fix spelling & punctuation" feature, the message text on your screen is sent over HTTPS directly to the AI provider selected in Settings — Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT), or Google (Gemini). Your API key is sent to that provider to authenticate the request. The developer does not receive your text or key. The provider processes the text and returns an edited message; its retention and data-use terms apply. Review the relevant provider's privacy policy:

  • Anthropic (Claude)
  • OpenAI (ChatGPT)
  • Google (Gemini)

If you don't use the Polish feature, RelayIQ does not send your message to an AI provider. Speech recognition and sharing with other apps are described below.

Speech recognition

When you tap Speak, RelayIQ opens the speech recognizer installed on your device. RelayIQ does not record or store audio itself. Your speech service may transmit audio to its servers according to its settings and privacy policy. RelayIQ receives the resulting text to display in the message box.

Local data storage

API keys and display preferences are saved in the app's private storage. API keys are excluded from Android backup and device transfer; display preferences may be backed up by Android. Keys are transmitted only to the selected provider when authenticating a polish request. Message drafts are held in memory and are not saved by RelayIQ.

Retention and deletion

Use Clear to remove the current message. You can remove a provider's key in Settings, or clear the app's storage in Android settings to delete all locally saved keys and preferences. Uninstalling removes the app's local data. The developer holds no server-side copy to delete. For data processed by an AI provider, speech service, or messaging app, use that service's privacy controls or contact that service.

Sending messages

When you tap Send, RelayIQ opens your phone's own Messages, WhatsApp, Messenger, Teams, or Email app (or a general share sheet) with your text pre-filled. You choose the recipient and send it yourself — RelayIQ does not send anything automatically and has no visibility into who you send it to.

Advertising and subscriptions

RelayIQ does not currently show ads or offer a paid subscription. If we add advertising (via Google AdMob) or a subscription (via Google Play Billing) in the future, this policy will be updated first to name the specific services used and what data they collect, and the update will be dated at the top of this page.

Children's Privacy

This Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
