Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

KaylDownUnder built the RelayIQ app as a free app. This Service is provided by KaylDownUnder at no cost and is intended for use as is.

What RelayIQ does with your data

RelayIQ does not have its own server, does not create user accounts, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to KaylDownUnder.

The one exception is the optional "Fix spelling & punctuation" feature. If you choose to use it, the message text currently on your screen is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you've selected in Settings — Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT), or Google (Gemini) — using an API key you provide yourself. RelayIQ never sees or stores that text; it is sent straight from your device to that provider and back. Review the relevant provider's own privacy policy for how they handle it:

  • Anthropic (Claude)
  • OpenAI (ChatGPT)
  • Google (Gemini)

If you don't add an API key or don't use the Polish feature, no message text ever leaves your device through RelayIQ.

Local data storage

RelayIQ has no accounts and no server-side storage. Your API key(s) and display preferences (text size, font, colour) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. API keys are specifically excluded from Android's automatic backup and device-transfer features, so they never leave your device even when your phone backs up or transfers to a new one — each install starts blank, and you add your own key.

Sending messages

When you tap Send, RelayIQ opens your phone's own Messages, WhatsApp, Messenger, Teams, or Email app (or a general share sheet) with your text pre-filled. You choose the recipient and send it yourself — RelayIQ does not send anything automatically and has no visibility into who you send it to.

Advertising and subscriptions

RelayIQ does not currently show ads or offer a paid subscription. If we add advertising (via Google AdMob) or a subscription (via Google Play Billing) in the future, this policy will be updated first to name the specific services used and what data they collect, and the update will be dated at the top of this page.

Children's Privacy

This Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
